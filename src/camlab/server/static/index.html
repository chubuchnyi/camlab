what these seven numbers are

7 free parameters: position, orientation, focal. grey = derived, not solved. Principal point is a parameter of each camera file, not a constant: this clip is cropped off-centre, so its optical axis sits 638 px outside the frame, and four of the five solves in runs/fan were nonetheless fitted at the image centre. A camera is only valid under its own — scoring one against another's is measuring a camera nobody solved. No distortion term, and measurement says one is not needed: real markings bow 0.37 px.
