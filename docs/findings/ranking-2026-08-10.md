# Every camera we have, through the validated metric

Measured 2026-08-10. Same frames, same detector, same metric, `worst offset` = the worst
marking's perpendicular distance from the detected line it corresponds to.

| model | worst, median | p90 | matched | missed | distinct positions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A per-frame, shipped | 54.0 px | 157.9 | 91 | 37 | 120 |
| B PTZ, old objective | 152.2 px | 222.9 | 85 | 46 | 1 |
| **C per-frame + line refit** | **24.3 px** | **94.1** | 91 | 37 | 120 |
| D PTZ + line refit | 75.1 px | 157.8 | 86 | 45 | 1 |
| **F #119 rigid (broadcast clip)** | **4.4 px** | | | | 1 |

## What it says

**Refitting against the right objective is worth 2–3×, on identical correspondences.** A→C is
54.0 → 24.3 px with the same 91 matched and 37 missed, so none of it was bought by measuring less.
B→D is 152.2 → 75.1 px. Both fits had converged on nearest-paint ICP, which does not measure
camera error; pointing them at what is actually judged moved them a long way for a median of
0.24 m and +12 px of focal.

**The PTZ model is still twice as bad as the per-frame one, and that is now a real comparison.**
Before the refit, B's 152 px said nothing — it was a fit against the wrong objective. After it, D
at 75.1 px against C's 24.3 px is a like-for-like statement: **on this clip, one shared position
costs a factor of three.**

That is not the same as saying a fixed camera is wrong. D's shared centre **never moved from its
seed** — 4.80, −75.05, 22.85 m before and after — which is the same symptom the earlier paint
sweep showed and diagnosed: the objective is flat along the viewing direction because focal and
distance trade off on a plane. So D is one position, but not a *fitted* one, and the factor of
three is what per-frame freedom buys against a position nobody has managed to determine.

**F is the scale, and it is sobering.** A camera known to be right reads 4.4 px on another clip.
C, the best thing this repo has produced, is 24.3 px — **five and a half times worse**. The refit
found a better camera near the old one; it did not find a right one.

## What the numbers do not settle

- D's focal spans 300 to 20000 px, both search bounds, so some frames are degenerate and the
  shared-centre fit is being asked to accommodate them.
- The centre not moving is unexplained by anything except the known flatness. A shared position
  fitted against a flat objective is a seed with extra steps.
- 37 markings of 128 go unmatched in A and C. Those are not scored at all, and a camera cannot be
  ranked on the part of the pitch nobody measured.
